Join Us

Please read the section below that applies to you before writing — it tells you what I need to hear and what I don't.

Postdocs

Send a brief email to bedartha.goswami@iiserpune.ac.in, along with a CV of no more than two pages.

PhD students

PhD admissions run through the Department of Data Science's own process — see the department's admissions page. Apply there; I review applications through that process and don't have a separate channel for reaching me directly.

MS thesis students

IISER Pune students only

Before writing, look at the Research page for the kind of problems the group works on, and talk to current group members (see People) about what a project looks like day to day.

When you do write, keep it short: include a copy of your transcript. Instead of a project pitch, it suffices to let me know in one line what interests you.

Semester project students

IISER Pune students only

Same process as MS thesis students above: check the Research page and talk to group members first, then a short email with your transcript and whether you lean toward coding or theory.

Summer project students

IISER Pune students only

Same process as above.

If you're not at IISER Pune, see the IISER Pune Summer Student Programme for how to apply as an external summer student.

Interns

I'm not accepting interns at the moment — the group is already at capacity for informal/internship positions.
