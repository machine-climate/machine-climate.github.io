Research interests

Developing stable, physics-constrained machine learning emulators to replace computationally demanding cloud simulations in super-parameterized climate models, for long-term extreme weather forecasting. Also integrates these fine-scale climate predictions with agent-based economic models to evaluate their socioeconomic impacts.
